Brazil tax reform consulting for companies

Soléo Business Consulting helps companies that operate in Brazil, including subsidiaries of foreign groups, understand how the new consumption taxes (CBS and IBS) affect pricing, contracts, credit and cash flow. The service is 100% online, in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

What is changing and when

PeriodWhat happens
2026Test year: CBS 0.9% and IBS 0.1% shown on invoices and offset against PIS and Cofins. Simples Nacional companies are outside the test.
Aug 3, Oct 1 and Dec 1, 2026Invoice fields for CBS and IBS required for NF-e, NFC-e and CT-e (Aug 3), NFS-e (Oct 1) and other documents (Dec 1).
Jan 1, 2027CBS charged at the reference rate minus 0.1 percentage point; PIS and Cofins extinguished; IBS at 0.05% plus 0.05%; IPI zeroed except for Manaus Free Trade Zone products. Simples Nacional documents must carry the new fields.
2027 onwardSplit payment: tests since Oct 15, 2026, availability in the second half of 2027, optional and business-to-business; the mandatory date is undefined.
2029 to 2032ICMS and ISS reduced to 90%, 80%, 70% and 60% while IBS grows.
2033ICMS and ISS extinguished.

Dates follow official guidance available on Nov 11, 2026 and may be adjusted by new rules. A TCU estimate released on Oct 28, 2026 points to a CBS reference rate of 11.3%; the final rate depends on a Senate resolution. The sum of the CBS and IBS reference rates is capped at 26.5% by LC 214/2025.

Where the reform reaches your business

  • Pricing and margin: how CBS, IBS and credits change the price you can charge and the margin you keep.
  • Contracts: adjustment, tax pass-through and neutrality clauses in long-term agreements.
  • Credit and cash flow: credit on purchases and the effect of split payment on working capital.
  • Invoicing and systems: invoice fields, tax classification and rejections.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to speak Portuguese to use the service?

No. Meetings and reports are available in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

Does the reform affect foreign companies that operate in Brazil?

Companies that carry out operations with goods or services in Brazil are within the scope of CBS and IBS. The effect depends on the activity, the tax regime and the contracts.

What are the CBS and IBS rates?

In 2026 the test rates are 0.9% for CBS and 0.1% for IBS, offset against PIS and Cofins. In 2027 CBS is charged at the reference rate minus 0.1 percentage point; a TCU estimate released on Oct 28, 2026 points to a reference rate of 11.3%, but the final figure depends on a Senate resolution.
