CBS and IBS simulator (Brazil)

Estimate how CBS and IBS affect tax on sales, credits on purchases and price, using the 2026 test-year and 2027 estimate scenarios. Free educational tool; it does not replace your accountant's tax computation.

How to use the simulator

  1. Choose the scenario: 2026 (test year), 2027 (estimate) or custom.
  2. Enter monthly sales before tax and purchases with CBS and IBS shown on the invoice.
  3. If the activity has a rate reduction, select 30% or 60%.
  4. Read the result and compare scenarios by changing one field at a time.

Assumptions and limits

  • The 2027 CBS rate uses the estimate released by Brazil's Federal Court of Accounts (TCU) on 28 Oct 2026 (reference rate of 11.3%) minus 0.1 percentage point, i.e. 11.2%; IBS is 0.1% (0.05% state plus 0.05% municipal). The final rate depends on a Senate resolution.
  • Tax is calculated on top of the net price, and credit assumes the full amount entered for purchases. In practice, credit depends on documents and specific rules.
  • The simulator does not include ICMS, ISS, IPI, the Selective Tax, specific regimes or the DAS computation.
  • In 2026 the amount shown on the invoice is offset against PIS and Cofins, and Simples Nacional is outside the test.

Frequently asked questions

Does the simulator show the amount I will actually pay?

No. It is an educational estimate with simple assumptions. The actual computation depends on the regime, how products and services are classified, the credits allowed and the rules in force. Check it with your accountant.

Why is the 2027 CBS rate shown as 11.2%?

The TCU estimate released on 28 Oct 2026 points to a CBS reference rate of 11.3%. In 2027 CBS is charged at the reference rate minus 0.1 percentage point, which gives 11.2%. The final figure depends on a Senate resolution.

Does the simulator include ICMS and ISS?

No. ICMS and ISS remain in force, with gradual reduction from 2029 to 2032, and are extinguished in 2033. The simulator covers only CBS and IBS.

Can I test other rates?

Yes. Change the CBS and IBS fields; the scenario becomes custom and you can compare hypotheses.
