Highlight · contract analysis

Contracts signed today will live through the reform

Contracts with customers and suppliers signed now will remain in force throughout the 2026–2033 transition. If they do not provide for changes in taxes, prices and payment methods, the margin is exposed and the discussion turns into conflict.

We analyze the company's contracts and propose adjustments so that they absorb future changes in prices and in taxation, preserving the balance between the parties.

What we review

1
Price and adjustment. Indexes, frequency and triggers tied to tax changes.
2
Pass-through of taxes. Who absorbs the replacement of PIS, Cofins, ICMS and ISS by CBS and IBS.
3
IBS and CBS credits. Rules to preserve credit along the chain and on the invoice.
4
Split payment. Payment terms and conditions with tax withheld at settlement.
5
Rebalancing and review. Clauses to renegotiate if the tax burden changes.
6
Supplier's tax regime. Impact of buying from suppliers under Simples, Hybrid Simples or the Regular Regime.

Frequently asked questions

Before booking

Does the service have any cost?

No. The intake, the 50-minute meeting and the tailored report are with no obligation and no cost. If the company wants to proceed with implementing the measures, we present a separate proposal.

What information do you ask for at intake?

Only your name, the company details (name, city, line of business, tax regime, revenue range), your agenda and your day and time preferences. We do not ask for personal ID numbers or personal data.

I already have an accountant. Does the service replace accounting?

It does not replace it: it complements it. The report brings business decisions (tax regime, prices, contracts, credit, cash) that can be shared with your accountant for execution.

How does the meeting take place?

By Google Meet, phone call or WhatsApp video, as you prefer. It lasts 50 minutes and is scheduled within 5 business days, based on the three day and time options given at intake.

What does the report include?

The company's needs mapped out from the intake and the meeting, the list of possible solutions and the necessary measures, organized by priority. It is delivered within 3 business days after the meeting.

Where do you provide service?

Service is national and international, 100% online, in Portuguese, English and Spanish, by Google Meet, phone call or WhatsApp video.
